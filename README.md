## 🌟 The 5-Point Character Sheet
1. **Name:** Nebupip ("Pip")
2. **What they are:** A thumb-sized cosmic sprout baby born from a fallen star settled in celestial cloud-moss. Wrapped in an oversized nebula swaddle with large curious liquid eyes that track the visitor and a glowing star-antenna.
3. **What they do:** Apprentice Starcharter & Dream-Weaver. Harvests drifting nightmare static and spins it into warm pastel bedtime dreams and cozy lullabies.
4. **Their story:** Born during the Great Aurora Snooze when a star sneezed into a moonbeam pillow. Now on a journey across the retro constellation web to deliver 10,000 gentle dreams before sunrise.
5. **Tagline:** *"Even the smallest star lights up the biggest dream!"*
